## Required behavior

- Moves, Abilities, Features and Edges must offer separately selectable source-backed original/non-errata and revised alternatives wherever applicable. Retain each evidenced alternative. Label source, edition/date and status accurately: an optional playtest is not automatically official errata because of its directory or chronology. No invented original for a revision-only addition, invented errata copy, arbitrary source blending or Generation 9 expansion.
- Variants have distinct stable document identities and an evidenced shared canonical relationship. Catalog coordinates and displayed names are not runtime identity. Explicit acquisition/replacement selects a version; its description, prerequisite report, calculations and automation agree. Standard PTU 1.05 remains the new-world baseline. World switches and presets do not replace individual saved selections.
- Features and Edges, including the distinct Poké Edge type, must be addable to supported trainer and Pokémon characters despite unmet game-rule prerequisites. An authorized addition needs no special prerequisite-bypass approval. Display visible, persistent, itemized **non-blocking** diagnostics identifying the item, selected source/version, every known unmet requirement, and separately every unverifiable/custom requirement. Satisfied requirements must not be reported missing; ambiguous conditions must not be guessed satisfied or unmet.
- Evaluate the owned/selected record's complete raw prerequisites, not browser chips, a same-name record or the latest default version. Preserve stable skill keys and accepted aliases. Missing actor fields, ambiguous identities and textual GM-approval requirements without evidence remain explicitly unverifiable. Re-evaluate diagnostics when relevant actor/item inputs change and after reload; retain raw source requirements.
- No supported add route may retain a contradictory hard game-prerequisite rejection or silently raise skills/add dependencies to suppress warnings. Warning-only acquisition must not disable an item solely for unmet prerequisites. Ordinary ownership/security, supported-content scope, actual effect-use conditions, explicit cancellation and legitimate choice semantics remain separate. This does not make an undefined effect safe to automate.
- Preserve saved/custom content, IDs, provenance, selections and relevant actor state. Explicit variant replacement preserves applicable state, reports prerequisites without blocking, and prevents alternate versions silently stacking the same benefit. Preserve legitimate repeatable/ranked choices; do not introduce a blanket duplicate ban. Ambiguous legacy records require visible review without forced conversion or deletion.
